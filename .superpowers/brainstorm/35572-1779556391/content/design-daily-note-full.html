What each section stores
1. Scan Coverage
Universe size, scored count, %, cycles, real data count
2. All Scored Symbols
Every symbol that ran through scoring — full table, not just top picks. The complete picture.
3. Skip Reasons
Why each non-scored symbol was dropped. Tracks data quality trends over time.
4. Rotation Diagnostics
Fresh discoveries vs repeats, universe coverage %. Is the bot seeing enough of the market?
5. Top Signals
Curated Tier 1/2/3. The bridge to Command Center pulls only from here.
6. Outcomes
Target/stop hits, active count, P/L. Performance record per day.
7. Signal Scorecard
Rate breakdown by setup. Accumulates into learning data over days/weeks.
8–10. Self-Review, Suggestions, Strategy
Tony's assessment, rule suggestions with confidence levels, strategy version tracking.
Why this enables learning
After 20 trading days, Claude can read across the vault and answer: "which setups have been hitting targets?", "is rotation coverage improving?", "what skip reasons are growing?" The full data — not just curated picks — is what makes this a real memory layer.
