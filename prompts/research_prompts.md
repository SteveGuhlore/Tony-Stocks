# Research Prompts

## Strategy research

```text
Research [strategy idea] for stock trading.
Explain:
1. the hypothesis,
2. entry/exit rules,
3. common indicators/parameters,
4. risks,
5. how to backtest it,
6. reasons it may fail.
Do not claim it will be profitable.
```

## Broker research

```text
Research [broker/API] for a paper-trading stock bot.
Cover:
1. paper trading availability,
2. Python SDK,
3. data access,
4. order types,
5. rate limits,
6. costs,
7. risks/limitations.
Prefer official docs.
```
